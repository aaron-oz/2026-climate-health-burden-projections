Abbreviations used throughout: TMREL (theoretical minimum risk exposure

* Summary

1. The measured ERA5 daily-temperature uncertainty is small: pop-weighted
   per-country sd has median 0.47 °C (range 0.23 to 0.77 °C) under the
   correlated intra-day bound and median 0.18 °C (0.10 to 0.29 °C) under the
   independent bound, both for calendar year 2022. The 1.0 °C scalar tested
   earlier was 2 to 5 times too large.
2. Convolving the replica exposure with the measured field moves the global
   burden by +0.4 % (correlated bound) or +0.1 % (independent bound); the
   median country moves +0.5 % / +0.1 %. A small set of hot, narrow-spread
   countries moves more (Malaysia +22 %, Liberia +31 %; micro-burden islands
   show larger ratios on denominators of a few deaths).
3. Against GBD 2019, adding measured noise to the pairing-fixed burden (B)
   changes little: countries inside the published UIs go from 143 to 140 of
   184, the tropical low cluster from 14 to 15 of 21, previously-in-UI
   defectors from 26 to 30. It neither clearly helps nor clearly hurts.
4. The +7 % unattributed residual of the pairing fix is essentially NOT
   explained by the two remaining reconstructable approximations: IHME's
   whole-degree TMREL rounding accounts for -0.3 % globally, draw-specific
   mortality weights for -0.01 % (exact for ischemic heart disease, which
   dominates the weights; a stress bound perturbing all 17 causes stays
   within -0.04 % at the median). The residual is documented as external
   (IHME's subnational aggregation and GBD-round internals).
5. The pipeline now has both fixes behind config flags
   (=TMREL_MODE=derived_per_draw=, =TEMP_NOISE_MODE=era5_sd=), verified
   byte-identical to production behavior at default settings, with derived
   TMRELs cached per (location, year) so the production-rerun overhead is
   about +1 % wall-clock rather than +30 %.
6. The flagged pipeline reproduces the replica's predictions on a
   10-location validation set (section [[*Step E: local validation of the flagged pipeline]]).

* Step A: the measured uncertainty field

IHME's =era2melt.R= added daily exposure noise with sd equal to ERA5's
published analysis-uncertainty field: the spread of the EDA, a 10-member
assimilation ensemble published 3-hourly at 0.5°. Our CCKP/NEX-GDDP input
has no uncertainty field, so production carries no such term. Step A
measured the field itself:

- Downloaded: dataset =reanalysis-era5-single-levels=, product
  =ensemble_spread=, variable 2 m temperature, all of calendar 2022,
  3-hourly, 0.5°, via the CDS API (12 monthly netCDFs, about 1.5 GB total,
  in =~/data/wb-temp-attr-projections/era5_sd/raw/=).
- The published spread is 3-hourly; the pipeline needs the uncertainty of
  the DAILY MEAN, which depends on the unknown intra-day error correlation.
  Two bounds are carried through everywhere:
  - =sd_corr= = mean of the day's eight 3-hourly spreads (errors fully
    correlated within a day; also the closest reading of "IHME applied the
    published field as-is").
  - =sd_indep= = sqrt(mean of squared spreads / 8) (errors independent
    within a day). Truth lies between the bounds; they differ by a factor
    of about 2.6.
- Aggregated to a per-pixel MONTHLY climatology (so winter/summer spread
  differences survive), bilinearly regridded 0.5° to the CCKP 0.25° grid:
  =era5_sd/era5_t2m_spread_daily_clim_2022_cckp025.nc=, symlinked at
  =data/era5_sd/=. Build scripts: =output/review-ssp245/fetch_era5_spread.py=
  and =build_era5_sd.py= (committed).

Pop-weighted per-country statistics (weighting each pixel-day by its
population share, 2022, 184 countries with land pixels at 0.25°):

| bound    | min  | p25  | median | p75  | max  |
|----------+------+------+--------+------+------|
| sd_corr  | 0.23 | 0.36 | 0.47   | 0.58 | 0.77 |
| sd_indep | 0.10 | 0.13 | 0.18   | 0.21 | 0.29 |

All values in °C; the maximum in both rows is South Sudan. The 1.0 °C
scalar previously tested (and rejected as a free dial) exceeds the
correlated-bound median by 2.1x and the independent-bound median by 5.5x.

* Step C: residual decomposition

B sits about +7 % above GBD 2019 globally (B/A = 1.066; median country
ratio 1.07 vs 0.97 under A). The two reconstructable candidate
explanations were tested in the same replica run:

- C.1, whole-degree TMREL rounding (IHME =pafCalc_sevFix.R:94= rounds;
  we keep the ERF grid's 0.1 °C): variant Br gives global Br/B = 0.9968
  (-0.3 %), median country -0.5 %. Rounding also happens to raise the
  in-UI count to 147 (from 143) and lower defectors to 23 (from 26).
- C.2, draw-specific TMREL weights (IHME weighted each RR draw's curve
  with the matching CoD draw's cause shares; we used fixed point shares):
  tested two ways. Exact: cvd_ihd (ischemic heart disease, the dominant
  weight) has real IHME forecast mortality draws for 168 of the 184
  countries (converted locally from the 6 GB forecast CSV); substituting
  draw d's cvd_ihd deaths into draw d's weights gives Bw/B median 0.9999,
  minimum 0.9949. Stress bound: perturbing ALL 17 causes' weights per draw
  with a seeded lognormal at cvd_ihd's measured relative draw-sd gives
  Bs/B median 0.9996, minimum 0.9835. Draw-specific weights are therefore
  immaterial to the level (they only widen the TMREL draws slightly).

Together the two explain well under 1 percentage point of the +7 %. The
remainder is documented as external: IHME's subnational (admin-1)
aggregation, their GBD-2019-round CoD draw weights (not reconstructible),
and other round-internal differences. Per the standing framing, GBD 2019
is a legitimacy check, not a calibration target, so the residual is not
chased further.

* Step D: pipeline implementation

Committed on =uncertainty-draws-fix= (global-scripts changes):

- =TMREL_MODE= config flag: =released_recycled= (default; production
  behavior to date) or =derived_per_draw=. The derived mode implements
  IHME's =tmrelCalculator.R:126= definition inside =02_load_tmrel.R=:
  TMREL draw d = argmin over 6.6 to 34.6 °C of ERF draw d's death-weighted
  RR curve, weights from the location-year's mortality cause shares,
  PER-DRAW when the mortality input carries draws (production does: the
  IHME forecast files have 500 draws) and point otherwise.
  =run_location.R= now runs 04 (mortality) before 02 (TMREL) so the
  weights are available; the order is inert in released mode.
- Derived TMRELs are cached per (location, year) under
  =data/tmrel/derived_cache/= because they do not depend on model or
  scenario; each cache file stores the weight rows it was derived from and
  is invalidated automatically if the mortality input changes. Measured on
  Colombia: first derivation +27 s, cache hit +3 s on an 82 s combo, so a
  full 27-model grid pays the derivation once per year, about +1 %
  wall-clock overall.
- =TMREL_ROUND_WHOLE= flag (default FALSE = keep 0.1 °C) reproduces
  IHME's whole-degree rounding if wanted.
- =TEMP_NOISE_MODE= config flag: =none= (default) or =era5_sd=, which
  makes =03_load_temperature.R= convolve each pixel-day's population mass
  with the measured per-pixel-month sd (variable chosen by =TEMP_SD_VAR=,
  =sd_corr= or =sd_indep=) before zone truncation. This is the
  distribution-level equivalent of IHME's noisy exposure draws, with no
  draw dimension and no RNG.
- Both runners (=util_run_cckp_burden.R= and Caspar's driver
  =util_run_global.R=) forward all new flags to their child processes.
- Safety: defaults verified byte-identical to the pre-change pipeline
  (max row difference 0 on the Colombia 2010 draws-mode burden);
  =util_verify_install.R= still passes 10 of 11 checks with the only FAIL
  the machine-expected missing CCKP mirror. Known limitation: noise mode
  outputs the exposure aggregated to temperature bins, so the diagnostic
  =util_trace_pipeline.R= (which expects pixel-day rows) does not support
  it, and =COLOMBIA_VERIFICATION= mode refuses it explicitly.

* Decisions requested

Answer by number; 1 and 2 are the substantive rulings, 3 is a
confirmation. All three only gate the wording of the Caspar rerun email;
the code supports any combination via flags with no further work.

1. *Daily-temperature noise: include in production, or document only?*
   - Option (a), document only (*recommended*): the production rerun uses
     =TEMP_NOISE_MODE=none=; the report documents that the measured ERA5
     EDA spread implies a bounded omitted effect of +0.1 % to +0.4 % on
     the global burden (independent / correlated intra-day bounds), with
     the largest country effects around +20 to +30 % in a few hot,
     narrow-spread, mostly small-burden countries. Rationale: the effect
     is far below the other uncertainties carried; the intra-day
     correlation choice (a 2.6x spread in the sd) cannot be resolved from
     published data, so including it forces exactly the kind of
     under-determined choice the review has been removing; and applying
     ERA5's ANALYSIS uncertainty to CCKP/NEX-GDDP bias-adjusted model
     output is conceptually shaky in the first place (the CCKP daily field
     is not an ERA5 analysis; its own error is unquantified).
   - Option (b), include with the correlated bound (=sd_corr=): most
     faithful reading of IHME's method (they did add exposure noise, and
     the published field is what they used). Costs: the bound choice is
     ours, in-UI agreement worsens slightly (143 to 140), and every future
     scenario run carries the convolution (sub-second per combo,
     negligible).
   - Option (c), include with the independent bound: nearly
     indistinguishable from (a) in results (+0.1 % global); mostly
     signaling that the term exists.
   - Deferring costs nothing: the flag can be flipped in any later run;
     temperature conversions are unaffected either way.

2. *TMREL resolution: keep 0.1 °C, or round to whole degrees like IHME?*
   - Option (a), keep 0.1 °C (*recommended*, and Aaron's stated lean):
     faithful to the argmin definition on the ERF grid. Costs: global
     burden sits 0.3 % higher than the whole-degree variant, and the
     GBD-2019 in-UI count is 4 countries lower (143 vs 147). Under the
     standing framing (GBD is a legitimacy check, not a target), matching
     IHME's discretization artifact to gain in-UI count would be
     calibration by the back door.
   - Option (b), whole-degree (=TMREL_ROUND_WHOLE=TRUE=): replicates
     IHME's released behavior exactly; the +4 in-UI countries and -0.3 %
     global are the entire upside.

3. *Confirm the production rerun config*: =TMREL_MODE=derived_per_draw=
   with the per-draw mortality weights that mode picks up automatically
   from Caspar's existing 500-draw mortality files (shown immaterial to
   levels, section [[*Step C: residual decomposition]], but faithful to IHME's
   method and free), =N_DRAWS=500= unchanged, temperature conversions and
   summarizer flow unchanged. Burden step is the only recompute.

* Limitations and parked items

- The sd field is a single-year (2022) climatology. EDA spread depends on
  the observing system, which changes slowly; 2022 is representative of
  the CCKP-era present, but a multi-year climatology would be a cheap
  upgrade if the noise term is ever included.
- The intra-day error correlation of the EDA spread is unknown; the two
  bounds differ by about 2.6x and IHME's exact aggregation in
  =era2melt.R= is not recoverable from the released code.
- 16 of the 184 replica countries lack local cvd_ihd forecast draws (the
  local 6 GB CSV extract covers 187 IHME locations); their C.2 exact test
  fell back to point weights. Production's own mortality files on
  Caspar's machine are the authoritative set; where a location's files
  lack draws the pipeline falls back to point weights with a log line
  rather than failing.
- The +7 % residual's external attribution (IHME subnational aggregation,
  round internals) remains unverifiable without their internal data, as
  the handoff anticipated. Parked items from the handoff (sub-grid island
  countries, zone-28 truncation sensitivity, GBD-2023-round curves)
  remain parked.
