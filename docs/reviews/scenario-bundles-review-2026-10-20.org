Deaths per year. Growth from 2022 to 2050 combines warming with growth in
the forecast all-cause deaths; I have not separated the two.

- ssp585 vs ssp245 (27 common models), 2041-2050: heat ratio 1.128
  globally; every one of the 27 models above 1 (range 1.04 to 1.25); cold
  ratio below 1 in all 27 (0.95 to 0.99). The ratio grows with time
  (decade means 1.01, 1.06, 1.13). Per location, median-over-models heat
  ratio 0.99 to 1.86, median 1.19. Expected direction and divergence.
- ssp370 vs ssp245 (24 common models), 2041-2050: heat ratio 1.006
  globally, cold 1.003; per-model heat ratio 0.90 to 1.08, 13 of 24 models
  above 1. So the two are close to indistinguishable before 2050. This is
  consistent with my recollection (not re-checked) of IPCC AR6, where
  SSP2-4.5 and SSP3-7.0 differ by roughly 0.1 C of global warming for
  2041-2060, with SSP3-7.0 aerosols offsetting early warming. It is not
  evidence of a bug, but it also means ssp370 results to 2050 carry little
  scenario signal beyond ssp245.
- In ssp370, Western and Central European locations (e.g. Austria 75,
  Germany 81, Czechia 47, Netherlands 89) have lower heat than ssp245 in
  most models (median ratio 0.88 to 0.98). Unexplained; I suspect regional
  aerosol forcing or model noise, unverified.
- Scenario ordering, 2041-2050 heat, 24 common models: ssp585 is the
  highest of the three globally in all 24 models; strict 245 < 370 < 585
  holds in 13 of 24 (the rest have ssp370 below ssp245). Per
  (location, model) pair: ssp585 > ssp245 in 89 %, ssp585 > ssp370 in
  80 %, ssp370 > ssp245 in 65 %.
- 2022 ratios are near 1 (heat 0.97 to 0.99, cold 1.006 to 1.007), as
  expected since scenarios diverge only from 2015.
- YLL per non-optimal death in 2050: 13.32 (ssp245), 13.34 (ssp370),
  13.48 (ssp585).
- Shock-tail locations (draw mean vs median of national non-optimal deaths
  differ by more than 10 %): the same locations as ssp245, led by Haiti
  (114) and Singapore (69), with Honduras (129) present. Nothing new.
- By cause, the heat ratios for =cvd_cmp= (2.97 for ssp585, 0.74 for
  ssp370) are ratios of near-zero, sign-changing totals (share of heat
  deaths about 0.000 to -0.002) and carry no information.
