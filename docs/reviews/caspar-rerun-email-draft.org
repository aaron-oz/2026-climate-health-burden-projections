Subject: ssp245 rerun: small pilot first, then the full burden pass

Hi Caspar,

Following up on the August 20 email: the uncertainty-draw pairing fix is
implemented, validated, and merged to main, and the open methodological
questions are settled (the updated review PDF is attached; addendum II
has the details). The ssp245 burden step needs a rerun. The temperature
conversions are untouched and fully reusable, so this is a burden-only
pass, and to be careful with your compute we would like to run it in two
stages: a small pilot (about 400 combos, roughly 0.3 percent of the
grid) that we check against precomputed expectations, then the full grid
once it passes.

One new flag to know about: --force_burden=TRUE. It forces recomputation
of the burden phase only; the conversion phase keeps its normal
skip-if-exists behavior. Nothing else about your commands changes; the
fix itself is the code default now, so there is nothing to configure.

Stage 1, the pilot (steps in order):

1. Update the repo: git pull on main. You should land on or past commit
   8f660e7.

2. Capture the OLD run's draw-level values for the pilot subset BEFORE
   they are overwritten (about a minute; writes one small CSV):

   Rscript output/review-ssp245/pilot_rollup.R pilot_draws_old.csv

3. Run the pilot: 15 locations, ssp245 only, year 2022 only, all
   models:

   parallel -j 15 \
     'Rscript global-scripts/util_run_global.R --location_id={} --scenarios=ssp245 --years=2022 --force_burden=TRUE' \
     ::: 6 11 13 81 102 114 125 131 135 145 163 171 190 213 214

4. Capture the NEW values:

   Rscript output/review-ssp245/pilot_rollup.R pilot_draws_new.csv

5. Send us back:
   - pilot_draws_old.csv and pilot_draws_new.csv,
   - a tarball of data/tmrel/derived_cache/ (small; it holds the newly
     derived TMRELs),
   - one or two run_2022.log files from
     output/results/cckp/<loc>/<model-scenario>/ (any pilot location),
   - a rough note of wall-clock for the pilot.

We will check the pilot against precomputed expectations on our side
(value-level predictions for the access-cm2 model, TMREL levels against
IHME's released values, draw-distribution behavior, timing) and reply
with a clear go / no-go.

Stage 2, after our green light: the full grid, your original command
shape plus the new flag:

   parallel -j 125 \
     'Rscript global-scripts/util_run_global.R --location_id={} --scenarios=ssp245 --years=2022-2050 --force_burden=TRUE' \
     ::: $(Rscript -e 'cat(readRDS("output/intermediate/ihme_loc_map.rds")$loc_id)')

then the summarizer and review-bundle export exactly as before. This
pass also sweeps up the three stale combos from the August 14 check, and
it recomputes the pilot combos' remaining years.

Cost expectation: per-combo time should match your original burden pass
to within a few percent. The one new computation, deriving TMRELs per
draw, is cached per location and year (it does not depend on model or
scenario), so it runs once per location-year rather than once per combo;
measured on our side it adds about 1 percent overall.
