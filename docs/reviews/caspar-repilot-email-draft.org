Subject: Re: ssp245 rerun: pilot result (FAIL was correct), short re-pilot, then the full grid

Hi Caspar,

Thanks for the pilot files and the timing note. Your checker's FAIL was
right, and stopping before stage 2 was the correct call. What it caught:

- 14 of the 15 pilot locations match our predictions within 0.7 %, and
  the draw distributions behave as expected (negative draws gone,
  intervals comparable to GBD 2019's).
- Haiti did not. In 22 of its 500 draws the IHME forecast for
  inj_disaster (deaths from exposure to forces of nature) is
  earthquake-scale (187,000 to 305,000 deaths, the 2010 event
  recurring), which makes disaster 81 % of those draws' cause mix. The
  death-weighted risk curve then has its minimum at the bottom of the
  search range (6.6 C), and every cause in those draws gets a heat PAF
  near 1. Nothing was wrong on your side: re-deriving Haiti's TMRELs
  here from your cache reproduces your values exactly. The per-draw
  weighting inherited an input tail our validation had not exercised.

The fix, merged to main as commit da35279, excludes inj_disaster from
the TMREL death weights everywhere; the cause still gets its
attributable burden like every other cause. Where the disaster share is
normal the change is below 0.01 C in the TMREL. It is the code default,
so there is nothing to configure. Two tools also changed: the roll-up
writes one extra column, and the checker now compares interval widths
against GBD 2019's rather than the old run's and checks that no TMREL
sits at the search floor. Both must be re-run from the updated main.

Please repeat stage 1, then go straight to stage 2 on a pass. Steps in
order, all from the repo root:

STAGE 1 (pilot, about 45 min at -j 15 going by your last run)

1. Update the repo and confirm you have the fix:

   git pull
   git log --oneline -1 da35279

   The second command should print the merge commit. If it errors with
   "unknown revision", the pull did not bring main up to date; stop and
   email me.

2. Keep your existing pilot_draws_old.csv. Do NOT re-run the roll-up to
   re-create it: the old run's values are no longer on disk, and the
   file you already have is the one we need.

3. Re-run the pilot, same command as before:

   parallel -j 15 \
     'Rscript global-scripts/util_run_global.R --location_id={} --scenarios=ssp245 --years=2022 --force_burden=TRUE' \
     ::: 6 11 13 81 102 114 125 131 135 145 163 171 190 213 214

   The TMRELs are re-derived once per location under a new cache file
   name, data/tmrel/derived_cache/<loc>_2022_N500_ex-inj_disaster.rds.
   The previous cache files (without the _ex- suffix) are left in place
   and ignored; no need to delete them.

4. Re-capture the new values with the updated roll-up:

   Rscript output/review-ssp245/pilot_rollup.R pilot_draws_new.csv

   This overwrites your previous pilot_draws_new.csv, which is intended.

5. Run the checker:

   Rscript output/review-ssp245/pilot_check.R pilot_draws_old.csv pilot_draws_new.csv

   Same verdict rules as before:
   - ALL PASS: go directly to stage 2 below, no need to wait for us.
   - WARN but no FAIL: go to stage 2, and email us the output in
     parallel.
   - Any FAIL: stop and email us. In particular, a FAIL that mentions a
     "pre-exclusion cache file" means step 1 did not take.

6. Either way, please email us for the record: pilot_draws_new.csv,
   the checker output, and a tarball of data/tmrel/derived_cache/
   (the new files end in _ex-inj_disaster.rds).

STAGE 2 (full grid, only after a PASS or WARN in step 5)

7. The full burden pass, your original command shape plus the flag:

   parallel -j 125 \
     'Rscript global-scripts/util_run_global.R --location_id={} --scenarios=ssp245 --years=2022-2050 --force_burden=TRUE' \
     ::: $(Rscript -e 'cat(readRDS("output/intermediate/ihme_loc_map.rds")$loc_id)')

   Temperature conversions are reused; only the burden phase runs. This
   also recomputes the pilot combos' remaining years and sweeps up the
   three stale combos from the August 14 check. Scaling your 45-minute
   pilot linearly to the full grid gives on the order of a day and a
   half at -j 125; we do not know how well the parallel efficiency
   holds at that width, so please note the actual wall-clock.

8. The summarizer, with --force this time. The summary tables gained
   columns (a draw median beside every mean, and non-optimal totals over
   the causes other than inj_disaster), and the per-location summary
   cache under output/summary/_cache/ will otherwise serve the old
   columns:

   Rscript global-scripts/util_summarize_run.R --scenarios=ssp245 --jobs=16 --force

9. The review-bundle export, exactly as before:

   Rscript global-scripts/util_export_review_bundle.R --scenarios=ssp245 --jobs=16

10. Email us the bundle, plus the wall-clock for steps 7 and 8.
