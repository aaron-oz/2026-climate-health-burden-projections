Subject: ssp585 test on 12 locations, including the scaling step

Hi Caspar,

Following today's meeting: before launching the other scenarios in full,
we would like one small end-to-end test of SSP5-RCP8.5 (ssp585) on 12
locations, carried all the way through the scaling step (Workflow B,
which rescales IHME's ssp245-based mortality by the ratio of our
ssp585 and ssp245 temperature signals). That step has not yet been run
on your machine, and we would rather find any problems on 12 locations
than on 204.

The 12 locations, chosen to cover climates, sizes and regions, mostly
reusing the pilot locations whose ssp245 results we know well:

| id  | location      | why                                               |
|-----+---------------+---------------------------------------------------|
| 163 | India         | Samuel's request; hot, largest population         |
|   6 | China         | large, cold north to subtropical south            |
| 102 | United States | largest compute of the set; cold-dominated        |
|  81 | Germany       | temperate Europe                                  |
| 135 | Brazil        | large, tropical to subtropical                    |
| 125 | Colombia      | validation country; we can re-run it here         |
|  11 | Indonesia     | equatorial islands, near-constant temperature     |
| 145 | Kuwait        | extreme heat                                      |
| 214 | Nigeria       | West Africa, large population                     |
| 190 | Uganda        | equatorial highlands                              |
| 114 | Haiti         | the known shock-tail case; tests the scaling there |
|  14 | Maldives      | micro-nation, tiny grid                           |

Everything is in one new script, run_ssp585_test.sh, with three steps.
From the repo root:

1. Update the repo:

   git pull

   Besides the new script, this brings a correction to the scaling
   formula (util_workflow_b_ratio.R): it used the attributable fraction
   itself where the method calls for the temperature scalar
   1 / (1 - attributable fraction). Nothing you have run used it yet.

2. Run the burden pipeline for ssp585 on the 12 locations:

   ./run_ssp585_test.sh run

   This is run_production.sh restricted to the 12 locations and ssp585,
   with the same preflight checks. It runs all 29 models x 29 years
   (2022 to 2050). It uses the settings in your run_env.sh (mirror paths
   and so on) except three it sets for this test: 12 locations at once,
   ssp585 only, and 4 burden workers per location (48 combos at once,
   fewer than the 80 in the ssp245 run; override with BURDEN_WORKERS=2
   in front of the command if memory gets tight). Watch it with
   ./status.sh. If it stops for any reason, run the same command again
   and it picks up where it left off.

   Rough estimate, not measured: about half a day to a day, set by the
   United States (its ssp245 burden step summed to about 40 hours of
   combo time; with 4 workers that is about 10 to 11 hours, plus the
   temperature conversion). Disk: about 300 GB of pipeline output
   (about 30 MB per combo, 10,092 combos).

3. When step 2 has finished, run the scaling:

   ./run_ssp585_test.sh scale

   This should take about an hour (estimate: 3.6 s per combo measured
   here, about 840 combos per location, the 12 locations in parallel).
   It scales ssp585 against your existing ssp245 run, and also
   scales ssp245 against itself for 2022 and 2050, which must reproduce
   the unscaled ssp245 numbers exactly (a built-in check). The last line
   printed for each location will report 58 "missing-ref" combos:
   hadgem3-gc31-mm and taiesm1 have ssp585 data but no ssp245, so they
   cannot be scaled (2 models x 29 years). That is expected. Anything
   marked "fail" is not. Disk: about 110 GB (about 11 MB per scaled
   combo).

4. Summarize and pack:

   ./run_ssp585_test.sh summarize

   Rough estimate: 10 to 30 minutes, most of it reading temperature
   files for the sanity checks described below. This summarizes the unscaled ssp585 output (same QA report as for
   ssp245, including the provenance check), then the scaled output (a new
   summarizer, util_summarize_workflow_b.R, with its own QA report), and
   packs both, the manifests, and one combo's raw files (Colombia,
   access-cm2, 2022) into:

   output/ssp585_test/ssp585_test_bundle.tar.gz

   Please send us that file. We expect roughly 40 to 100 MB (estimate).

The scaled-output QA report has two parts. The first is arithmetic that
must hold; a failure there is a bug. The second is a set of sanity checks
on whether ssp585 behaves like a warmer world than ssp245, comparing the
two runs model by model: population-weighted temperature (ssp585 should
be about equal in 2022 and warmer by 2040 and 2050), heat deaths up and
cold deaths down by the 2040s, models that warm more showing larger
heat-death increases, the scaling changing totals by a few percent at
most, and the heat share of the burden rising. Those are marked CHECK
rather than FAIL, because a real result can break a rule of thumb. If
you see CHECK lines, no need to do anything; we will look into them.

On our side we will also check that our own runs of Colombia
(access-cm2, 2022 and 2050, both scenarios) match yours. We ran the whole
sequence here on those combos: every arithmetic check passed, and our
ssp245 values match your round-2 table exactly. (The sanity checks need
many models to mean anything, so one model tells us little there.) So we do not expect surprises, but that
is one location and one model, not 12 and 29.

If anything fails, the logs to send are under output/logs/ (the newest
dated folder for step 2, the ssp585_test_scale_* folder for step 3).
