| Location    | dT 2040 | dT 2050 | Models warmer 2050 | Heat ratio 2041-2050 | Models with more heat |

So the lower ssp370 heat burden in these four countries does not come with
lower annual mean temperatures: they are about 0.1 C warmer on average,
with models split roughly evenly. The temperature data here do not explain
the heat result. Possible reasons, none tested: annual means can hide
differences in summer extremes, which drive heat deaths; the four sampled
years may not represent the decade; or this is model noise (the heat ratio
uses all ten years, the temperature only two). Settling it would need
summer or hot-day temperature summaries per model, which only Caspar's
machine has.
