* Finding 1: Haiti, derived TMRELs collapse to the search floor in 22 of 500 draws

Per-location value gate (access-cm2, deaths from non-optimal temperature,
2022, mean over 500 draws): Caspar's new run matches our replica-B
prediction within 0.7 % for 14 of 15 locations (China, Indonesia,
Malaysia, Germany, United States, Colombia, Brazil, Kuwait, India, DR
Congo, Uganda, Niger, Nigeria; Nicaragua at +3.6 %). Haiti: 2,536 vs
predicted 684.

Haiti's draws: median 391, but 22 of 500 draws (indices 28, 36, 40, 101,
111, 142, 184, 205, 213, 227, 298, 344, 348, 359, 368, 397, 423, 425,
438, 447, 460, 495) give 56,000 to 90,000 deaths, heat-dominated. The
same 22 indices blow up in all 27 ssp245 models, so the cause is a
per-draw input shared across models.

His cache shows it: in those 22 draws the derived TMREL is 6.6 C (the
floor of the 6.6 to 34.6 C search range) in nearly every zone, against
17 to 27 C in the other 478 draws. The cache's stored cause-of-death
weights (=cod= table) show why: Haiti's IHME forecast draws for
=inj_disaster= (deaths from exposure to forces of nature) have median 35
deaths in 2022 but 187,000 to 305,000 deaths in those 22 draws
(earthquake-scale; 4.4 % of draws). In those draws disaster deaths are
81 % of the 17-cause mix, so the death-weighted RR curve is essentially
the =inj_disaster= curve, whose argmin is at the floor. The floored
TMREL then applies to all 17 causes in that draw, so IHD, stroke, LRI,
etc. get heat PAFs near 1 on top of the disaster deaths themselves.

Cross-checks:
- Re-deriving Haiti's TMRELs locally from his =cod= table and our
  500-draw ERF cache reproduces his cache exactly (all 11,500 values).
  His machine and ours agree on the code; the disagreement with our
  expectations is entirely the input tail.
- IHME's released TMREL draws for Haiti show the same collapse in 2010,
  the earthquake year: mean TMREL 6.7 to 9.6 C across most zones, 74 % of
  draws below 10 C, vs about 17 to 25 C in 1990 and 2020. The derivation
  is faithful to IHME's method including this artifact. The difference
  is that IHME used realized deaths for one year, while our inputs are
  forecast draws in which a 2010-scale event recurs in about 4 % of
  draws in every projection year.
- The memo's step C.2 stress test (lognormal perturbation of all causes
  at cvd_ihd's relative draw spread) could not represent a 4 % point
  mass at 5,000 times the median, so this mode was outside what we
  tested.
- Nicaragua is a milder case: max =inj_disaster= share 38 % in some
  draws, no floored draws, TMREL mean abs diff 0.07 C, burden +3.6 %.
  Indonesia's max share is 17 %, no visible effect.

Excluding the 22 floored draws, Haiti's access-cm2 mean is 396 (median
391, 95 % interval 271 to 575), against GBD 2019's 416 (306 to 562);
across the 27 models the range is 320 to 456. The old run's mean of 159
was pulled down by negative draws (min -13,771) from the pairing bug.

The width gate compares new to old 95 % interval width per combo. The
failures are Indonesia, Malaysia, Uganda, and 2 of 27 DR Congo combos
(ratios 0.27 to 0.53, uniform across each location's 27 models) plus all
Haiti combos (ratios up to 27, finding 1). Relative widths (95 %

| location  | old  | new  | GBD 2019 |
|-----------+------+------+----------|
| Indonesia | 2.95 | 0.92 |     0.60 |
| Malaysia  | 4.57 | 1.15 |     0.82 |
| Uganda    | 2.81 | 0.61 |     0.51 |
| DR Congo  | 1.80 | 0.93 |     0.56 |
| Brazil    | 0.64 | 0.37 |     0.22 |
| China     | 0.35 | 0.34 |     0.30 |

The narrowing moves the tropical locations toward, not below, GBD 2019's
relative widths, and the old widths there were several times GBD's. This
is consistent with the pairing bug having inflated the old widths (the

we have no independent prediction of the new widths (step E kept only
means), so this reading is plausible rather than verified. The gate's
baseline should be changed rather than the result.

* Aaron's first ruling and its implementation (2026-09-14, morning; SUPERSEDED below)

Aaron's ruling: exclude =inj_disaster= from the derived-TMREL weights
everywhere (not per location). Rationale, in order of weight: the
exclusion is measurably free where the disaster share is normal (12 of
15 pilot locations: mean TMREL shift 0.000 to 0.010 C, re-derived from
Caspar's own weights); the tail is not Haiti-only (Nicaragua max
per-draw share 38 %, Indonesia 17 %) and the other 189 locations cannot
be screened locally; the =inj_disaster= curve has no interior minimum, so
its weight can only drag the TMREL toward the floor; and a global rule
is one sentence of methodology where a per-location exception is a
judgment call per location and year. Recorded as decision 4 in
[[file:uncertainty-draws-decision-memo.org]].

Implementation (branch =tmrel-exclude-disaster=): =TMREL_WEIGHT_EXCLUDE=
in config.R (default =inj_disaster=; =--tmrel_weight_exclude=none=
disables), applied to both the per-draw and the point-weight paths in
=02_load_tmrel.R=; the exclusion is stored in the cache object and in the
cache file name (=<loc>_<year>_N500_ex-inj_disaster.rds=), so Caspar's
existing pre-exclusion cache files are never reused; both runners forward
the flag.

Local verification, Haiti and Nicaragua, access-cm2 2022, mortality
input built from Caspar's own per-draw cause weights (his cache's =cod=
table, single age-sex bin):
- exclusion OFF reproduces Caspar's pilot draw-for-draw (max
  difference 1e-10 deaths) and the derived cache exactly;
- legacy =released_recycled= reproduces his OLD run draw-for-draw
  (4e-11), so nothing outside the derived path moved;
- exclusion ON: Haiti non-disaster burden mean 391, median 385, 95 %
  interval 271 to 548 (GBD 2019: 416, 306 to 562), no zone-draw at the
  floor; Nicaragua 279.4 vs replica 279.3 (ratio 1.0003).

Checker revised (=pilot_check.R=; see the memo's decision 4 for the gate
list) and exercised on a synthetic post-fix pilot (Caspar's 14 non-Haiti
locations plus the local Haiti run): all gates pass except a WARN on
Nicaragua that is an artifact of reusing his pre-exclusion Nicaragua
draws in the synthetic file, and which the local Nicaragua run shows

* The inj_disaster cause's own burden in shock draws

Independent of the TMREL, in Haiti's 22 earthquake-scale draws the
=inj_disaster= cause's attributable burden is a PAF of about +/-1 to 2 %
(heat +0.019, cold -0.024 at the point estimate) applied to 190,000 to
305,000 deaths, i.e. +/-2,000 to 11,000 deaths per draw, of either sign.
This swings Haiti's TOTAL non-optimal-temperature burden far more than
the other 16 causes combined (total mean 286, median 381, 95 % interval
-1,649 to 574 under the exclusion; the old run had the same feature,
range -13,771 to 6,803). It is IHME's forecast uncertainty propagating
through a small PAF, not a pipeline defect, and it is unchanged by this
branch. It does mean that for Haiti (and any location with a comparable
shock tail) the draw MEAN is a poor summary of the total.

Aaron's ruling (2026-09-14): keep the mean as the point estimate (it adds
across causes and locations, and it is what the GBD 2019 centrals are
compared against; for the 14 non-Haiti pilot locations median/mean is
0.968 to 1.004, so the choice only bites where a shock tail exists), and
make the alternative summaries available rather than switch estimator.
=util_summarize_run.R= now writes, in =national_by_year.csv=, a =_median=
next to every =_mean=, and =deaths_nonopt_exdis= / =yll_nonopt_exdis=
(mean and median over the causes outside =TMREL_WEIGHT_EXCLUDE=); its
=qa_report.txt= gains a shock-tail section listing combos whose
|mean - median| / |median| exceeds =--shock_gap= (default 0.10). On the
local runs: Haiti flagged (gap 0.25; total mean 286, median 381,
non-disaster mean 391), Nicaragua not (0.01). How the report presents
the flagged locations is still a write-up decision, but the numbers it
needs are in the bundle. The summarizer's per-location cache must be
rebuilt (=--force=) for the new columns to appear.

* Team decision (2026-09-14 afternoon, Aaron, Caspar, Charlie): keep all 17 causes

The team voted to stay as close to Burkart et al. and IHME as possible:
all 17 causes weight the derived TMREL everywhere. Haiti's estimates get
an asterisk; the team will either rerun Haiti alone with 16 causes
(=--location_id=114 --tmrel_weight_exclude=inj_disaster=, which writes
its derived cache under a distinct file name) or keep it as-is and
footnote the earthquake-scale =inj_disaster= forecast draws.
