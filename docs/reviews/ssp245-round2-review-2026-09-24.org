Usable after a four-combo fix. 159,728 of 159,732 combos were recomputed

* Finding 1: four combos failed and the tables silently kept their old values

The burden manifest (=manifests/cckp_burden_manifest.csv=, an append log
of every combo attempt) records the stage-2 run between 2026-09-16 12:36
and 2026-09-17 15:07 on Caspar's machine clock (time zone not recorded),
about 26.5 hours. In that window, 159,728 combos have status =ok=. Four
have status =fail= with message "Rscript exit 137" and no later retry:

| location        | id  | model      | year | killed at (manifest clock) |
|-----------------+-----+------------+------+----------------------------|
| Netherlands     | 89  | access-cm2 | 2022 | 2026-09-16 14:08           |
| Costa Rica      | 126 | access-cm2 | 2022 | 2026-09-16 20:09           |
| Montenegro      | 50  | access-cm2 | 2022 | 2026-09-16 21:32           |
| Canada          | 101 | access-cm2 | 2022 | 2026-09-17 00:15           |

Exit 137 is a SIGKILL, typically the kernel's out-of-memory killer; the
bundle has no stage-2 log to confirm that (see finding 7). That all four
are the first combo of their location (access-cm2, 2022) is consistent
with a memory peak at location start-up, but this is a reading of the
pattern, not verified.

Because the old output files were still on disk, the summarizer read
them. Verified: these four are exactly the combos whose
=deaths_nonopt_mean= is identical to round 1 (difference below 1e-6
deaths), and exactly the four combos (of 159,732) whose burden did not
rise. They also match the old-method replica (replica A, from the
2026-08-20 all-204 test) to within 0.03 %, while the new-method replica
(replica B) predicts 3.6 % to 15.9 % more:

| location    | table value (= round 1) | replica A | replica B | B / table |
|-------------+------------------------+-----------+-----------+-----------|
| Montenegro  |                  337.2 |     337.3 |     356.9 |     1.058 |
| Netherlands |                5,340.7 |   5,342.4 |   5,658.8 |     1.059 |
| Canada      |                8,535.8 |   8,535.8 |   8,855.2 |     1.037 |
| Costa Rica  |                  251.5 |     251.4 |     291.3 |     1.159 |

(Deaths attributable to non-optimal temperature, mean over 500 draws.)

Effect on the 27-model ensemble mean for 2022: +0.14 % (Canada) to
+0.47 % (Costa Rica) once fixed, estimated as (B/table - 1) x table / 27
/ ensemble mean. Small, but access-cm2 2022 is the combo every
validation comparison uses, so the fix is worth the few minutes it costs.
A draft email with the fix-up steps is in
=docs/reviews/caspar-round2-fixup-email-draft.org=.

Why nothing flagged it: the summarizer's QA checks arithmetic, shape,
and coverage of the files on disk; it does not compare against the
manifest, so a stale file from an earlier run passes. A check that the
latest manifest status of every summarized combo is =ok= (and newer than
a given date) would have caught this. Implemented 2026-09-25 on this
branch: =util_summarize_run.R= now reads the burden manifest
(=global-scripts/util_run_provenance.R=), reports a Provenance section in
=qa_report.txt= (FAIL when a combo's latest non-skip attempt is not =ok=,
or, with =--since=<date>=, when it has no =ok= attempt since that date),
writes the flagged combos to =provenance.csv=, and writes =fix_run.sh=
with the rerun, cache-clearing and re-summarize commands. On this
bundle's manifest it flags exactly the four combos above (with
=--since=2026-09-16=, nothing else); tested end to end on a synthetic
run with a stale, a not-recomputed and a missing combo.

* Finding 6: shock tails and other mean-median gaps

The summarizer's QA lists 29 locations with at least one combo where
|mean - median| / |median| of national non-optimal deaths exceeds 0.10
(4,827 of 159,732 combos). Separating heat and cold gaps, and the gap in
the 17-cause mortality itself (=deaths_mean= vs =deaths_median=), sorts
them into four groups. Values below are 27-model means for 2022 unless
stated.

1. Haiti (the known case). Mean 2,405, median 370, 95 % interval 251 to
   41,691; paper era 416 (306 to 562). Heat mean/median 2,126/234. The
   =inj_disaster= share of mean 17-cause mortality is 17.0 %, against a
   median of 0.02 % across the 204 locations; the next highest is 2.3 %.
   All 783 combos flagged. As decided 2026-09-14: asterisk.

2. Honduras (new, same signature, milder). Mean 525, median 431,
   interval 335 to 1,803; paper era 422 (346 to 524), so the mean sits
   just above the paper UI and the median inside it. 555 of 783 combos
   flagged; the gap shrinks over time (heat mean/median - 1 is 0.80 in
   2022, 0.25 in 2050).

   Hypothesis: the Haiti mechanism, from a smaller disaster tail in
   IHME's =inj_disaster= forecast. Evidence, all from summaries
   (access-cm2, 2022 unless stated):
   - The skew is on the heat side only (heat mean/median 258/165, cold
     unskewed), which is what a TMREL pulled down in some draws produces;
     a death-count tail would skew heat and cold alike (as in Venezuela).
   - The 17-cause mortality mean exceeds its median by 773 deaths, and
     the =inj_disaster= mean is 726 deaths. So nearly all of the
     mortality skew is the disaster cause, with a median near zero and
     the mean carried by a tail. Haiti shows the same identity (excess
     9,906 vs disaster mean 10,428), Nicaragua a smaller one (165 vs
     228).
   - =inj_disaster= is 2.3 % of Honduras's mean cause-mix deaths, second
     only to Haiti (17.0 %) of 204 locations (median 0.02 %).
   - The 97.5th percentile of heat deaths is 1,691, ten times the median,
     so at least 13 of 500 draws are strongly affected.

   What is missing compared with Haiti: (a) no anchor event of Haiti's
   scale. Hurricane Mitch (1998) is the obvious candidate in IHME's
   history, and Eta and Iota (2020) a recent one; their death tolls are
   not checked here, and we have not seen what history IHME's disaster
   forecast draws on. (b) No IHME-side confirmation: the released TMRELs
   for Honduras (1990, 2010, 2020) show no collapse (0 of 100 draws below
   10 C in any year; mean 22.7 to 23.2 C), but none of those years
   follows a major Honduran disaster, so this neither supports nor
   refutes. (c) No direct view of the draws: the bundle has no Honduras
   derived-TMREL cache, so floored or lowered TMREL draws are not
   confirmed. Open puzzle: Nicaragua has a similar disaster share (1.7 %)
   but a far smaller effect (heat 97.5th percentile 1.6 x median vs 10 x);
   the per-draw shares would say whether Honduras's tail is concentrated
   in fewer, larger draws.

   Test: Honduras's derived-TMREL cache files (=data/tmrel/derived_cache/129_*=
   on Caspar's machine, which store the per-draw cause weights and
   TMRELs) allow the same diagnostics as for Haiti: count draws at or
   near the 6.6 C floor, their disaster share, and the burden with
   =inj_disaster= left out of the weights. Requested in the fix-up email.

3. Venezuela (a different mechanism: the mortality forecast itself). In
   2022 the gap is 4 %; from 2024 on it is 13 % to 20 %, in all 27
   models. The 17-cause mortality has the same gap (mean 16 % to 18 %
   above median from 2024, upper bound about 2.0 x median), so a subset
   of IHME's forecast draws carries much higher all-cause mortality and
   the burden scales with it. Mean IHD deaths in the access-cm2 inputs
   rise from 42,828 in 2022 to 62,251 in 2026. Not a TMREL effect and
   not a pipeline defect, but the same caveat applies to the mean.

4. Small-number locations. Singapore (mean 60, median 50, interval 23
   to 160; paper era 85 inside) has near-constant temperatures, so its
   net burden is sensitive to where each TMREL draw falls; both heat and
   cold are right-skewed, the mortality gap is under 0.5 %. The remaining 25
   locations in the QA list have small counts: 21 have under 12
   attributable deaths a year (Cook Islands 1.0, Samoa 2.5, Grenada
   2.3, ...), where a gap of 5 % to 10 % crosses the 0.10 threshold in
   some years; the four larger ones are Suriname (30 deaths, 1 flagged
   combo), Liberia (61, 14), Mauritius (109, 4) and Puerto Rico (111,
   14). (The QA floors the median at 1 death, so locations under 1 death
   a year, such as Tokelau, are not listed.)

Cold-country heat skews (Iceland, Ireland, UK, Nordic countries, heat
mean several times its median) reflect tiny heat counts and do not move
the totals (national mean-median gaps: median over combos under 2 %,
at most 4.4 %, in Moldova); none is flagged.

* Finding 7: bundle hygiene

- =manifests/joblog.tsv=, =verify.log= and =loc_172.log= are from the
  2026-08-14 run (timestamps inside them), not from stage 2. The export
  copies the newest directory under =output/logs/=, and the stage-2
  command wrote no logs there. So the bundle has no stage-2 run log; the
  manifest timings stand in for the wall-clock.
- No =deaths_nonopt_exdis= columns: correct, since the summarizer writes
  them only when =TMREL_WEIGHT_EXCLUDE= is set, and it is empty by team
  decision. (The 2026-09-14 pilot review describes them without that
  condition.)
- Hard QA checks all pass (PAF and death sums, |PAF| <= 1 with max
  0.686, all finite); coverage complete (27 models x 29 years for all
  204 locations); the Colombia normalized sample reconstructs exactly in
  all 324 sampled combos. The two CCKP models without converted
  temperatures (hadgem3-gc31-mm, taiesm1) are recorded as
  =missing-temp= for all 204 locations, as in round 1; they are not among
  the 27.
