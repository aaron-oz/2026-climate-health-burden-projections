#+TITLE: ssp585 12-location test: review plan and expectations, written before the results
#+DATE: 2026-09-28

What we will check when Caspar's =ssp585_test_bundle.tar.gz= arrives, and
what we expect to see, written down before seeing it so the expectations
cannot drift toward the results. Test setup: =run_ssp585_test.sh=, email
=docs/reviews/caspar-ssp585-test-email-draft.org=. Terms:

- Unscaled burden: the pipeline's attributable deaths for a scenario, IHME
  mortality x that scenario's PAF (PAF = population attributable fraction).
- Scaled burden (Workflow B): IHME mortality x scale factor x target PAF,
  with scale factor = (1 - PAF_ssp245) / (1 - PAF_ssp585) for the 13 causes
  IHME temperature-adjusts, and 1 for the other 4.
- Ensemble: mean over the 27 models that have both ssp245 and ssp585.

* 1. Arithmetic (hard checks)

Both QA reports' hard-check sections pass: the unscaled ssp585 report (same
checks as ssp245, including the provenance check that every combo was
computed in this run) and the Workflow B report (heat + cold = non-optimal,
no NA or non-finite values, scale factor exactly 1 for the 4 non-adjusted
causes, ssp245 scaled against itself reproducing the unscaled ssp245 burden
draw for draw, every row matched to a life table). Any failure is a bug.

* 3. Plausibility (the summarizer's section, plus our reading)

The Workflow B QA report now carries a plausibility section, flagged
CHECK rather than failed. Our expectations, per check:

- P1, temperature. Ensemble population-weighted temperature difference
  ssp585 - ssp245 near 0 in 2022 and positive, growing, by 2040 and 2050 in
  all 12 locations. Individual models can show either sign in any single
  year (each scenario run has its own weather), which is why the check is
  on the ensemble. We have not looked up the expected size of the 2050 gap
  for these countries; the check only asks for sign, growth, and no value
  above 2 C.
- P2, direction. Unscaled heat deaths higher under ssp585 by 2041-2050 in
  every location; cold deaths equal or lower. Near-identical in 2022-2030.
  The net (heat + cold) can go either way: in cold-dominated locations
  (Germany, United States, China) fewer cold deaths may outweigh more heat
  deaths, so a lower net under ssp585 there is not by itself a problem. In
  heat-dominated locations (Kuwait, India, Nigeria) we expect the net to
  rise.
- P3, coherence across models. Within a location, models whose ssp585
  warms more relative to their ssp245 should show larger heat-death
  increases: positive correlation across models at 2050.
- P4, size of the scaling. Scaled / unscaled within a few percent of 1,
  since the scale factor moves mortality by the change in 1/(1 - PAF).
  Haiti is the exception to watch: in its floored-TMREL draws PAF reaches
  about 0.7 (round-2 QA, max |paf_nonopt| 0.686), where 1/(1 - PAF) is
  steep, so its scale factors can spread further.
- P5, heat share of the burden higher under ssp585 by 2041-2050.
- P6, uncertainty widths similar to ssp245's.

Our own reading on top of the report:

- 2022 ssp585 ensemble vs the round-2 ssp245 ensemble for the same
  location: within a few percent, since the scenarios have barely diverged.
- Shape over time: smooth ensemble trajectories 2022 to 2050, no jumps
  between adjacent years.
- Haiti's shock tail present equally in both scenarios (the mortality
  draws are the same), so its mean-median gap should look like ssp245's.
- The two models with ssp585 but no ssp245 (hadgem3-gc31-mm, taiesm1)
  appear in the unscaled summary and as "missing-ref" in the scaled one.

* 4. What this test cannot tell us

- Whether ssp585 magnitudes are right in absolute terms: there is no
  observed ssp585 world to compare with. We have not found a published
  country-level SSP5-8.5 vs SSP2-4.5 temperature-mortality projection with
  our cause set and years to compare against; a literature check would be
  a separate task.
- Whether the Workflow B method itself is right (anchoring on IHME's
  ssp245 mortality and rescaling). The test checks that the code does what
  the method note says, not the method.
- Population: IHME's mortality counts carry IHME's reference-scenario
  population, so the scaled ssp585 burden does not reflect SSP5
  demography (known simplification, =docs/ops/pipeline-state-for-cckp.md=
  section 5.1).
