* Decided
- Periods (deck question 2): the decades 2022-2030, 2031-2040, 2041-2050,
  and 2022-2050 as a whole, for every table. One annual table in addition:
  all ages, both sexes, all causes combined, for every year 2022-2050, with
  the same contents as the decade tables (deaths and YLLs for heat, cold,
  heat + cold; mean and 95/80/66% ranges; difference from SSP2-4.5 for the
  other three scenarios; 204 countries, World Bank groups, world). No
  5-year periods.
- Age groups: 50-69 dropped. Kept: the 17 five-year groups, under 5,
  15-49, 15-64, 65+.
- Age standard (deck question 3): the IHME (GBD) world standard population
  only. WHO 2000-2025 is not computed.
- Table list (deck question 1): nothing missing; the list in the email of
  2026-11-16 stands, with the changes above.
- Vetting quantities: coarse only (country and group by decade), for our
  own checks; nobody else needs them.
- Historical data (deck question 7): no figures from us for now (others may
  make them). Our job is to put the historical estimates into the tables so
  they can be queried alongside the projections.
- Timing: aim to have the final pass done by Thanksgiving (2026-11-26).
  Caspar does not need to delete anything before the end of 2026.

- Historical table format (Aaron, after the meeting): deaths only (crude
  rate, age-standardized rate, percent of all deaths; no YLLs); World Bank
  groups built from countries with GBD 2019-round population if easy to get;
  same long table shape as the projections with a source column. Details in
  the table list, section C.
