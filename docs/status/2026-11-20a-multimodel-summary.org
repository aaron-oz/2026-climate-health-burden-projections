* Decisions made, and why
- Team, 2026-11-18 (relayed by Aaron): decades plus 2022-2050 for every
  table, plus one annual all-ages, both-sexes, all-causes table; no 5-year
  periods; age group 50-69 dropped; GBD (IHME) world standard population
  only; nothing added to the table list; vetting quantities coarse, for us
  only; historical estimates go into the tables (no figures from us);
  target done by Thanksgiving (2026-11-26); Caspar need not delete anything
  before end of 2026.
- Aaron, 2026-11-18: historical source the GBD 2019 round only (the 2023
  round is not comparable to our numbers); deaths only (crude rate,
  age-standardized rate, percent), World Bank groups via GBD 2019-round
  population, same long shape as the projection tables.
- Aaron, 2026-11-20: no valuations in this run; keep the compact copy so
  valuation work can later rebuild the draws. Valuation details belong to
  the valuations team (Jacx and Patrick Fahr), not to us.
- Aaron, 2026-11-20: Caspar runs both stages (we receive the tables and
  reports; the compact copy stays on his machine until hosting is settled,
  not us).
- Agent, 2026-11-19: two stages (compact copy, then tables from it) rather
  than one pass writing tables, so tables can be recomputed without another
  pass over the 10 TB; rebuild check on every file, not a sample.
- Agent: population-weighted temperature recorded at 2022, 2030, 2040, 2050
  only (vetting quantity; --temp_years restores every year at extra reading
  cost).
