- Aaron, 2026-11-13: CLASS_2026_10_08.csv and WBG_adm0.csv are the official
  classification; regions do not wait for another file.
- Aaron, 2026-11-13: reports and methods documents never name team members;
  only meeting summaries and contribution requests may (memory saved).
- Aaron, 2026-11-16: get every summary the team can think of computed as
  complete tables in ONE pass of Caspar's machine over his existing output
  (no model reruns), including age-standardized rates (ASRs), before the
  per-draw output is deleted. Reason: uncertainty ranges cannot be added up
  from summary tables (example: low and middle income heat deaths, SSP2-4.5,
  2041-2050, true 95% range 456,000 to 992,000 per year; summing country
  bounds gives 390,000 to 1,102,000; assuming independence 542,000 to
  798,000). Means can.
- Aaron, 2026-11-16: final tables are publishable numbers usable for
  monetary valuation; vetting quantities (model min/max, models above
  SSP2-4.5, attributable fraction, crossover year, population-weighted
  temperature) only at country/group by decade. Model-set sensitivity tables
  dropped (already checked; can be reported from 2026-11-06 output).
- Aaron, 2026-11-16 (in the sent email): periods are decades plus 2022-2050
  (annual or 5-year asked as a question); ages are 17 five-year groups plus
  under 5, 15-49, 15-64, 50-69, 65+; cause groups cardiovascular, diabetes
  and kidney, injuries, all causes except forces of nature; ASRs under both
  the GBD world standard and WHO 2000-2025.
- Agent finding, 2026-11-16: the full Caspar output (roughly 10 TB, scaled
  from his benchmark of about 52 GB per country) can be rebuilt exactly from
  a compact copy of about 50 to 70 GB (rough arithmetic): attributable
  deaths = IHME deaths (cause, age, sex, draw) x scale factor (cause, draw)
  x PAF (cause, draw), because PAF and scale factor are broadcast across age
  and sex (global-scripts/util_workflow_b_ratio.R lines 100-166). Verified
  exactly on the 4 local Colombia files only; must be checked on a sample of
  production files in the final pass before anything is deleted.
- Agent, 2026-11-16: historical anchor uses GBD's published estimates, no
  runs: the GBD 2019 round (Burkart et al. companion data, 1990, 2010, 2019;
  the round our method matches) and GBD 2023 (2019, 2022). Both on disk.
  There is no 2000 in the matching round (the sent email offers to download
  2000; it would come from the GBD 2023 round, which revised heat upward).
- Agent, 2026-11-17: the worked example uses a fresh real example rather
  than the older examples in docs/methodology/methodology-walkthrough.tex,
  which predate derived per-draw TMRELs and national weighting and use
  synthesized cross-scenario numbers.
