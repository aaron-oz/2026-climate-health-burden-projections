# Independent comparison: global pipeline (verification mode) vs Samuel's Colombia code

Produced 2026-05-04 by an independent code-review agent per the brief in
`handoff-pipeline-vs-samuel-comparison.md`.

**Mission:** Enumerate every behavioral difference between the global
pipeline (`global-scripts/`, `COLOMBIA_VERIFICATION = TRUE`,
`USE_DRAWS = FALSE`) and Samuel's original Colombia scripts
(`from-samuel/Scripts/Colombia/`) that could affect numerical output.
Qualitative enumeration only.

Items already settled by the handoff (TMREL source identity, converter
determinism, the SEV daily-summation bug, PAF flooring, RR rescaling,
TMREL averaging order, burden formula multiplier) are not re-litigated
here. Where the pipeline's `COLOMBIA_VERIFICATION` block is meant to
mirror Samuel but the mirroring is incomplete or differs in mechanism,
that gap **is** flagged.

In the "Plausible numerical impact" column:
- **Direction**: "closes" = bringing this in line would push pipeline
  toward Samuel; "widens" = away from Samuel; "unclear" = depends on
  data.
- **Magnitude**: large / moderate / small / negligible — order-of-magnitude
  judgement, no quantitative test.

Ordering is by line number in the global pipeline (config and orchestrator
first, then 01 → 08, then converter / YLL).

## Items confirmed equivalent (non-issues)

- **ERF cause mapping**: `util_convert_samuel_colombia.R:50-63` (17 entries) consistent with `09_curvas_ER.R:21-26` (alphabetical-order alias of 18 entries minus `inj_non_disaster`).
- **Daily-temperature precision**: both round to 0.1 °C (pipeline encodes as integer × 10).
- **Heat/cold classification of TMREL-equal days**: both drop them.
- **PAF formula**: identical `case_when(rr >= 1 ~ pr*(rr-1)/rr, ... ~ pr*-1*((1/rr)-1)/(1/rr))` in both.
- **TMREL source data**: byte-identical (per handoff).
- **Daily-temperature truncation to ERF bounds**: both truncate per zone.
- **Zone truncation 29-31 → 28**: both apply.
- **Mortality → 17 causes**: same total (884,628) confirms equivalent input through to the aggregation step.
- **Missing/NA handling in temperature data**: Samuel's RDS has no NAs; both pipelines see clean input.

## Suggested follow-up diagnostics (qualitative ordering)

The remaining numerical-attribution question (which of items 5/9/12/15/18/20-25/30-33 contributes how much) requires quantitative testing. If the gap needs to be narrowed further before quantitative follow-up, the highest-yield diagnostics would be (in suggested order):

1. **Recompute pipeline burden at depto × day granularity** (items 12, 15, 20, 21, 24, 25) and aggregate via Samuel's path. If the gap closes substantially, the depto-aggregation difference is the dominant cause.
2. **Patch `07_compute_ylls.R` to apply the SEV multiplier in COLOMBIA_VERIFICATION mode** (item 30). This will *widen* the YLL gap (pipeline YLL would drop further below Samuel's). The new gap, combined with item 31, gives a cleaner picture of the YLL-only divergence.
3. **Switch the converter to depto-level life tables** (item 32) and use Samuel's `(ex - 2.5)` / 10-year-cap rule (item 31).
4. **Re-derive `rr_max` using Samuel's max-of-99th-percentile definition** (items 5, 23) and re-run SEV.

## Summary

The dominant source of pipeline-vs-Samuel disagreement that is *not* already covered by the 5 known verification toggles appears to be the **structural aggregation level** (national-annual vs depto-day), captured by items 12, 15, 20, 21, 24, 25 — these constitute one architectural decision propagated through PAF, SEV, and burden multiplication.

After that, in rough qualitative magnitude order:

- Item 30: missing SEV in YLL pathway (verification-mode bug in pipeline)
- Item 31: pipeline uses `ex` directly; Samuel uses `(ex - 2.5)` and a 10-year cap for >80
- Items 5, 23: different `rr_max` definitions for SEV
- Item 18: PAF flooring point (per-row vs post-aggregation)
- Item 9: constant vs year-varying population
- Item 32: national vs depto life tables
- Items 14, 33, 13: smaller corrections (codptore=75 filter, age-0 vs age-1 in "0-4", San Andrés special-casing)

None of these are individually surprising; the cumulative effect of carrying ~7 small corrections plus one large structural decision is consistent with the ~30% total / ~50%+ heat-burden gap the handoff documents.

## Empirical findings established by R inspection

- Pop in `temperatura_diaria_pixel.rds` is constant per pixel across all 10 years (verified — 0 of 14,940 (pixel × year) cells show variation).
- `WorldPop_2010_2019_pixel.csv` (which Samuel uses) does vary per year for every pixel (1,493 of 1,493 with variation).
- Mortality file has 2,083 deaths assigned to codptore=75 ("EXTRANJERO") that Samuel filters out but the pipeline does not.
- DANE life table has both `cod_depto == "00"` (national) and depto-specific rows — pipeline uses national, Samuel uses depto.
- Total mortality across 17 causes = 884,628 (matches Samuel's reported denominator) with the codptore=75 deaths included.
